How it works — 3 steps

1. Dump fragments. Throw this week's work in, one item per line. Order and wording don't matter.

2. Tag status. Mark each item Done / In progress / Blocked. Blocked items are auto-routed to Risks — surfacing a problem in a report is an early warning; surfacing it at a missed deadline is an incident.

3. Generate and verify. The tool only restructures what you enter — it invents nothing. Spend one minute checking the numbers before sending.

Why "Progress / Risks / Asks"

Your manager isn't reading to learn what you did — they're scanning for: where things stand, what's stuck, and what needs their decision. This structure orders information by decision value so a 30-second scan captures everything.

If you can't fill those sections, it's usually not a writing problem — it's an unprocessed week. That's why this tool starts from raw fragments and lets structure do the sorting.

FAQ

Is my content uploaded anywhere?
No. This is a pure frontend page. Everything you type stays in your browser (localStorage draft) and auto-restores on your next visit.
How is this different from asking an AI chatbot?
AI chatbots send your content to a third party. This tool works offline with zero leakage — sensible for sensitive work content. They also combine well: structure here first, polish with AI second.
What happens to "Blocked" items?
They move to "Risks & Blockers" with your note attached. Proactively surfacing problems in a report is the cheapest credibility move there is.
